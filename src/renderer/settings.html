Focus

Settings are read-only during sleep hours.

Sleep hours

The screen locks during sleep hours. You get One More Minute once a night and the passcode once a night; after that, each 15 minutes needs a code sent over WhatsApp.

Entertainment mode

Entertainment sites and apps are blocked until you start Entertainment mode. Hitting one offers 25 minutes where only these work and other apps are hidden. Sites are covered in Safari, Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc, Vivaldi and Chromium. One per line.

Kid mode

stick.cafe is always blocked. Opening it offers 31 minutes of Kid mode, once a day, where only stick.cafe works.

Pomodoro

Start Pomodoro from the menu bar: 25 minutes on one ClickUp task, then a 5-minute break. Entertainment and Kid mode can't start during focus. Create a token in ClickUp › Settings › Apps.

Passcode

Tip: let someone else set the passcode, like Screen Time for a family member.

Someone you trust, with the country code. After the passcode is used, they get the codes from your WhatsApp account and decide whether to share them.
